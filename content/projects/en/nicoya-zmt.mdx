## Problem

The system is for the Maritime Zone department of the Municipality of Nicoya. It brings two things online: citizen paperwork and appointments, and the department's tracking of requests.

## My role

It was a university project at Universidad Nacional, in 2024 and 2025, built by a team of four or more people. My part was full stack.

## What I built

We built it as a team. It is an information system with two parts. A citizen portal, where people handle paperwork and book appointments online. An admin dashboard, where the department sees the state of each request in three categories: pending, approved and rejected.

The system emails citizens about their requests. It has tests, and the design works on phones.

React and Next.js build the interface, and Next.js also acts as the backend. The data lives in MariaDB, and the code is TypeScript.

## Key decisions

The team made these decisions.

### Separate citizen portal and admin dashboard

The team split the citizen portal and the admin dashboard into two parts, each with its own permissions.

### MariaDB, the database the Municipality already used

This was a constraint more than a choice. The Municipality already used MariaDB and gave us access to its database server, so we built the system on it.

### Appointments in fixed half-hour slots

Appointments use fixed 30-minute slots. The head of the department manages them from the dashboard and can block hours, so citizens do not see them.

## Results

The system is in production at [zmtmn-usuarios.municoya.go.cr](https://zmtmn-usuarios.municoya.go.cr) and real citizens use it. It replaced a process that was in person and on paper.
